## About the Tutorial

MultiFound focuses on recent advances in **multimodal foundation models for biomedical imaging**, bringing together methods that integrate medical images with complementary modalities such as text, clinical information, and other biomedical data.

The tutorial is intended to provide participants with an overview of the field, key methodological concepts, representative applications, and emerging research directions.

## Topics

The tutorial will cover topics related to:

- Multimodal foundation models for biomedical imaging
- Vision-language models in medicine
- Medical image and text representation learning
- Multimodal pretraining and alignment
- Adaptation and fine-tuning of foundation models
- Evaluation of multimodal medical AI systems
- Clinical and research applications
- Open challenges and future directions
